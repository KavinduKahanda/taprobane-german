FAQ

Questions, answered

Which book should I start with?

If you know no German, start with A1. If you can already introduce yourself and handle simple everyday situations, try A2. Each book ends with a final review and mock exam, so you can check whether you are ready for the next level.

Can I learn German alone, without a teacher?

Yes. Every book is a complete self-study course: grammar is explained in English, every exercise has an answer in the answer key, and AI prompts let you practise speaking and listening with free chat tools.

Are the books written for English speakers?

Yes. All explanations, instructions and grammar notes are in English; the German is introduced step by step.

Do the books prepare me for Goethe, telc or ÖSD exams?

Each book follows the CEFR level and includes an original mock exam in the style of a standard exam at that level, plus exam-skill training. The books are independent and not affiliated with any exam provider.

What is the difference between A1, A2, B1 and B2?

These are the levels of the Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR). A1 and A2 cover basic everyday German, B1 lets you handle most everyday and work situations, and B2 is the level often required for university study and skilled work in Germany.

Is there a Kindle version?

Yes. Every level is available as a paperback and as a Kindle eBook, and the Kindle editions are in Kindle Unlimited.

Is there a German course for nurses and caregivers?

Yes. German for Nurses & Caregivers is a separate series: Band 1 (B1 Pflege) for ward communication, Band 2 (B2 Pflege) for documentation and difficult conversations, and Band 3, an Exam Trainer.
