About the publisher

German books made for learning on your own.

German from Zero is the book brand of Taprobane Holdings, an independent publisher of self-study language books for English speakers.

What we publish

Two series, one method

Deutsch: German from Zero takes you from your very first word to B2 in four books.

German for Nurses & Caregivers teaches the German used on the ward, in care documentation and in nursing German exams.

Every book follows the same rhythm, so you always know what comes next: a real scene, grammar in plain English, graded exercises, AI speaking practice, and a review with a full answer key.

What you can rely on

Our promises

  • Every explanation is in English.
  • Every exercise has its answer in the answer key.
  • Independent: not affiliated with telc, the Goethe-Institut, ÖSD or any exam provider.
  • Honest reviews only — we never pay for, swap or reward reviews.
