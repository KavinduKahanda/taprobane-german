Who it is for

Complete beginners with no German at all — English speakers starting from zero.

Grammar covered

Present tense, sein and haben, gender and articles, plurals, word order and questions, the accusative, negation with kein, modal verbs, separable verbs, the imperative, time and place prepositions, an introduction to the dative, the Perfekt and weil-clauses.
