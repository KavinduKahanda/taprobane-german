Deutsch A2: German from Zero

A Complete Self-Study Course with Exercises, Mock Exam & Answer Key

Talk about what you did, where you live, your health, work and plans — and get ready for an A2 exam.

Who it is for

Learners who finished A1 (or know basic German) and want to talk about the past, plans and everyday life.

Grammar covered

Perfekt and Präteritum, the full dative system, two-way prepositions, adjective endings, comparative and superlative, reflexive verbs, dass/ob/wenn/als-clauses, relative clauses, infinitive with zu, um … zu, polite requests and advice with Konjunktiv II.
